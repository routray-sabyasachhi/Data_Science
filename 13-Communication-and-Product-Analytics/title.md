# Communication and Product Analytics

## Why study this
Data science supports decisions, so analysts must connect evidence to a real question and explain what action the evidence supports. Technical correctness without clear communication often has little impact.

## Topics and subtopics
- Problem discovery: stakeholder interviews, decision context, constraints, assumptions
- Metrics: metric trees, leading/lagging indicators, north-star metrics, guardrails, definitions
- Product data: events, funnels, cohorts, retention, conversion, segmentation, attribution limits
- Requirements and scope: feasibility, data availability, privacy, expected value, success criteria
- Analytical storytelling: context, question, evidence, recommendation, caveats, next steps
- Visual and written communication: clear charts, concise summaries, audience adaptation, accessible language
- Presenting uncertainty: ranges, scenarios, confidence, practical vs statistical significance
- Data documentation: metric definitions, dashboards, analysis notes, decision logs
- Collaboration: asking precise questions, review, reproducibility, disagreement with evidence
- Business reasoning: costs, benefits, tradeoffs, prioritization, monitoring outcomes after action
