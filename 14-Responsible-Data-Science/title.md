# Responsible Data Science

## Why study this
Data systems can affect people, communities, and organizations. Responsible practice anticipates harm, respects rights, communicates limits, and makes accountability part of the workflow.

## Topics and subtopics
- Ethics: consent, purpose limitation, proportionality, accountability, public interest
- Privacy: personal/sensitive data, minimization, de-identification limits, retention, access control
- Security: threat awareness, secure storage/transfer, credentials, incident response
- Fairness: protected groups, representation, measurement bias, disparate outcomes, tradeoffs, intersectionality
- Bias sources: collection, labeling, sampling, historical decisions, proxies, deployment context
- Explainability: stakeholder needs, global/local explanations, limits and misuse
- Governance: ownership, documentation, lineage, review, auditability, policies
- Legal awareness: applicable privacy, IP, consumer and sector rules; seek qualified counsel for interpretation
- Human factors: automation bias, contestability, human-in-the-loop design, recourse
- Reproducibility: versioned code/data, environments, seeds, experiment records, independent review
- Model/data documentation: datasheets, model cards, intended use, excluded uses, limitations
- Monitoring and retirement: harms, drift, complaints, incident response, decommissioning
- Generative AI risks: hallucination, prompt injection, leakage, provenance, copyright, misuse
