# Mathematics for Data Science

## Why study this
Mathematics makes model behavior, assumptions, optimization, and uncertainty understandable. Focus first on intuition and practical use, then deepen proofs as your goals require.

## Topics and subtopics
- Algebra foundations: equations, inequalities, exponents, logarithms, functions, composition, graphs, summation notation
- Linear algebra: scalars/vectors/matrices/tensors, shape, transpose, dot products, norms, distances, linear systems, rank, span, basis, independence, subspaces, orthogonality, projections, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, diagonalization, SVD, positive (semi)definite matrices
- Calculus: limits intuition, derivatives, partial derivatives, chain rule, gradients, Jacobians, Hessians, integrals, Taylor approximation, vector calculus basics
- Optimization: objective/loss, constraints, convexity intuition, gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent, momentum, learning rates, regularization, Lagrange multipliers awareness
- Probability mathematics: sets, counting, conditional probability, Bayes rule, random variables, expectation, variance, covariance, common distributions, law of large numbers, central limit theorem
- Discrete mathematics: logic, sets, relations, functions, combinatorics, graphs, trees, recurrence and complexity basics
- Numerical methods: floating point, precision, stability, conditioning, overflow/underflow, numerical differentiation
- Information theory: entropy, cross-entropy, KL divergence, mutual information
- Geometry for ML: distance, angle, hyperplanes, projections, high-dimensional geometry intuition
- Translating math to code: dimensions, vectorization, numerical checks, interpreting formulas
